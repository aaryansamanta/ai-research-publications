A study of the autonomic nervous system

Emotion may not be written in any one signal, but in how the signals lock together.

Skin conductance tells you how strongly someone is reacting. It cannot tell you whether the reaction is pleasant or unpleasant. Neither can heart rate alone, or breathing, or skin temperature. This project asks whether the missing information lives in the coupling between them.

Everything below runs on simulated physiology generated in your browser. The signals are synthetic; the analysis performed on them is the same analysis the study will run on real recordings.

03 What is being tested

Four statements, sealed before any analysis.

Two are primary and logically independent, so the study returns a finding whether or not coupling helps a classifier.

H1

Adding an explicit representation of coupling improves valence discrimination over a single-channel baseline.

Supporting
H2 ●

That improvement is larger for valence than for arousal. Arousal is carried well enough by single-channel intensity; valence depends on structure.

Primary · methodological
H3 ●

The learned connectivity differs systematically between states, and the direction of change agrees with established autonomic physiology.

Primary · physiological
H4

Pre-training on unlabelled recordings substantially reduces how much labelled data is needed for the same performance.

Supporting
Why two primaries. If coupling turns out not to help the classifier, H3 can still hold — the wiring may reorganise without that reorganisation being useful for prediction. That is a physiological result, and it is reported either way.

Coupling is the peak absolute lagged cross-correlation between the two feature series, searched over ±3 s. Computed live from the simulated traces, so the numbers move when the state does.
What to look for. Under stress the electrodermal and cardiac pair tightens while heart and breath come apart. Under amusement it reverses and the vasomotor pair — skin temperature with perfusion — comes alive. If measured data does this too, the wiring carries valence.

Model

A convolutional encoder turns each channel window into a node vector. The adjacency is initialised from the four measures above — a physiological prior — then refined by attention with a sparsity penalty, so the final structure stays readable. Two graph attention layers, pooled to a classification head.

The control that matters. The comparison baseline uses the identical channel set, window length and preprocessing with no coupling terms. Anything the graph gains over it is attributable to structure and nothing else.

08 Label efficiency

Physiology is cheap to record. Labels are not.

Every minute of signal costs nothing once the band is on a wrist. Every affect label costs a person stopping to rate how they feel. Pre-training on unlabelled recordings is how the study tries to trade one for the other.
