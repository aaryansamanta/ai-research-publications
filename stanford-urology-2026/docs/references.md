| 1 📎 | Miller JM, Ashton-Miller JA, DeLancey JOL. **A pelvic muscle precontraction can reduce cough-related urine loss in selected women with mild SUI.** *J Am Geriatr Soc.* 1998;46(7):870–874. DOI: [10.1111/j.1532-5415.1998.tb02721.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1532-5415.1998.tb02721.x) · PMID: [9670874](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9670874/) | [Local copy](../02-phase-2-imaging-analysis/reading/Miller-1998_JAGS_pelvic-muscle-precontraction-The-Knack.pdf). Source of "**The Knack**": a voluntary pelvic floor contraction timed before and during a cough. In 27 women with mild-to-moderate SUI, one week after learning it, leakage fell by an average of **98.2 %** (medium cough) and **73.3 %** (deep cough) versus coughing without it. |
