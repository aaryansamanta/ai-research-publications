## 🧬 Where the data come from

- The underlying ultrasound data are **anonymized** clinical data from controls and patients, **provided by the mentor** (individual subject records plus summary data from his publications and reports).
- The project **analyzed** these data; it did not collect them.
- The original subject records are held by the mentor and are **not** in this repository.
