## 🔬 Research notes & attribution

- **Published research vs. code:** A published paper or proposed architecture is not automatically equivalent to a fully reproduced implementation. In particular, the IEEE AIAM code demonstration emphasizes XGBoost/SHAP analysis; the GA–GNN paper methodology should be evaluated separately.
- **Collaborative work:** The UCLA COSMOS wildfire project was developed by a five-student team. The *C. elegans* work involved collaborators and mentors. Individual contributions should be documented in the corresponding repositories.
- **CogArc status:** A browser-based interactive prototype exists. Public-dataset evaluation, graph-attention model validation, and any wearable/EEG studies are **planned**, not completed clinical or scientific validation.
- **Clinical privacy:** The Stanford Urology work concerns mentored analysis of existing measurements. Patient-level clinical data should not be publicly exposed.
- **Social-impact projects:** AI Ethos and NextGenAI demonstrate organization and website/platform development. The sites do not by themselves establish which features Aaryan personally coded; the repositories should document specific contributions.
- **Kaggle results:** Medal placements and denominators are presented as supplied; verify against public competition profiles before application submission.
