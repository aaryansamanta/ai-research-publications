# Ethics / IRB Checklist

Pre-college research involving human subjects (especially minors) typically needs sign-off from your school, your mentorship program (e.g., MIT THINK), and — if you plan to enter science fairs (ISEF-affiliated) or submit to certain journals — a formal IRB (Institutional Review Board) or equivalent review. Track status here.

|---|---|---|

| Debrief/withdrawal procedure defined | ☐ Not started | How a student opts out mid-study without penalty |

## Notes for CBE—Life Sciences Education Submission

CBE (an ASCB journal) publishes education research involving human subjects; manuscripts typically need a statement confirming IRB approval or exemption, and a description of consent procedures, in the Methods section. Draft this language once your review status above is finalized, and cross-check current author guidelines on the journal's website before submission, since requirements can change.
