## Parental/Guardian Consent Form (Template)

**Study Title:** When Help Becomes a Hindrance: How Simplified Biological Diagrams Create Systemic Misconceptions

**Researcher:** [Student researcher name], [School], under mentorship of [mentor name/affiliation]

**Purpose:** This study looks at how different styles of biology diagrams affect students' understanding of biology concepts (such as cell structure or genetics). Your child's class may be asked to complete a short survey, a lesson using one of two diagram styles, and a brief follow-up quiz.

**Procedures:** Participation involves a short pre-lesson questionnaire, a normal classroom lesson (using either a standard or a redesigned diagram — students are not told which), and a post-lesson quiz/drawing task. Total additional time: approximately [X] minutes across [Y] class periods.

**Risks:** Minimal — comparable to normal classroom activities. No physical, financial, or significant psychological risk anticipated.

**Benefits:** Participants may gain additional learning-support materials. Findings may help improve how biology diagrams are designed and taught in the future.

**Confidentiality:** Responses will be anonymized/coded (no names retained on data used for analysis). Data will be stored securely and only accessible to the research team and mentor.

**Voluntary Participation:** Participation is voluntary. Your child may decline or withdraw at any time without penalty or effect on their grade.

**Contact:** For questions, contact [researcher email] or [mentor/school contact].

## Student Assent Form (Template — age-appropriate language)

**What is this about?** We're studying whether different styles of biology diagrams help people understand ideas like cells or genetics better.

**What will I do?** Answer some short questions before and after a normal class lesson, and maybe draw a quick diagram. It won't be graded — we just want to know what you think.

**Do I have to do this?** No. It's your choice. You can say no, or stop at any time, and nothing bad will happen.

**Will anyone see my name?** No — your answers will be kept private and not linked to your name in any report.
